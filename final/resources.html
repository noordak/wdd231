Food & Dining Near Disneyland

Staying near the Disneyland Resort gives visitors several convenient options for meals before and after a day in the parks.

McDonald's

Quick-Service Dining

McDonald's is a familiar option for a quick, casual meal. Visitors can find burgers, fries, breakfast sandwiches, coffee, and other convenient choices for a busy park day.

Location: 1500 S. Harbor Blvd., Anaheim, CA 92802

Visit McDonald's Website

IHOP

Family-Friendly Dining

IHOP is known for pancakes, waffles, omelets, and classic breakfast favorites. It can be a good option for families looking for a sit-down breakfast or a relaxed meal.

Location: 1560 S. Harbor Blvd., Anaheim, CA 92802

Visit IHOP's Website

Tips for Choosing a Restaurant

Compare menu options, parking fees, walking distance, seating capacity, and prices that match your budget.

Remember to check current rates and availability directly with each restaurant.

Restaurant hours, menus, and locations may change. Check the restaurant website before visiting.

Hotels Near Disneyland

Choosing a hotel close to the parks can make your vacation more convenient. Compare location, amenities, room options, and parking before booking.

Camelot Inn & Suites

Convenient Park Access

Camelot Inn & Suites is located across Harbor Boulevard from the Disneyland Resort entrance area. The hotel offers family room options, complimentary Wi-Fi, a pool, a whirlpool spa, and in-room refrigerators and microwaves.

Address: 1520 S. Harbor Blvd., Anaheim, CA 92802

Phone: (714) 635-7275

Visit Hotel Website

Desert Palms Hotel & Suites

Family-Friendly Accommodations

Desert Palms Hotel & Suites is located near Disneyland and across from Anaheim GardenWalk. Amenities include a complimentary hot breakfast buffet, family suite options, complimentary Wi-Fi, a fitness center, and a pool with a whirlpool spa.

Address: 631 W. Katella Ave., Anaheim, CA 92802

Phone: (714) 535-1133

Visit Hotel Website

Tips for Choosing a Hotel

Compare nightly rates, parking fees, walking distance, breakfast options, room capacity, and cancellation policies before making your reservation.

Remember to check current rates and availability directly with each hotel.
