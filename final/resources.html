Disneyland Trip Resources

Planning ahead can help your Disneyland vacation feel less stressful and more enjoyable. Use these resources to prepare for your visit.

Packing Checklist

Use this checklist to keep track of some common items your family may want for a day at Disneyland.

Food & Dining

Plan Ahead

Think about when your family usually gets hungry and plan meal breaks around your day.

Family-Friendly Options

Look for dining locations with menu choices that work well for the different preferences in your group.

Stay Flexible

Leave some flexibility in your schedule so meals don't become another source of stress.

Places to Stay

Disneyland Resort Hotels

Staying at a Disneyland Resort hotel can provide a convenient option for visitors who want to stay close to the parks.

Nearby Hotels

Hotels in the surrounding Anaheim area can provide additional choices for different budgets and travel needs.

Compare Before Booking

Consider location, parking, transportation, room size, and the needs of your family when choosing accommodations.
