Site Name

Disneyland Trip Planner

Site Purpose

I chose this name but it might be subject to change. The website will help those preparing for a trip to Disneyland. It will provide information in one place so visitors can spend less time searching for information and more time enjoying their vacation.

The website will include information about:

  • Popular Disneyland rides and attractions
  • Ride locations, types, and height requirements
  • Places to eat at and around Disneyland
  • Nearby hotels and places to stay
  • What to pack for a Disneyland vacation
  • General tips and resources for planning a family trip

Scenarios

The following questions represent things that visitors to the website might want to know when planning their Disneyland vacation.

  • What Disneyland rides are available for my children based on their height?
  • What should I pack for a day at Disneyland?
  • Where are some good places to eat during our Disneyland trip?
  • What hotels are located near Disneyland?
